Status: draft complete; evidence-linked review performed for manuscript V1.

This audit applies only to `MANUSCRIPT_V1.md` and its generated LaTeX counterpart. It supplements rather than replaces the project's existing final claims audit. “Supported” means the wording is bounded by a committed aggregate authority, implementation/configuration source, or verified literature-evidence record. It does not convert internal evidence into external validation.

## Audit conclusion

All major quantitative and methodological claims in manuscript V1 have an identified committed authority and are stated with their necessary boundary. No unsupported novelty, superiority, field-readiness, causal-diagnosis, physical-localization, or external-validation claim is approved. Author metadata remain deliberately unresolved placeholders. The most important interpretive risks are proxy-onset validity, conditional delay, FAR denominator confusion, internal-versus-external evaluation, and misreading the project-specific policy freeze as a standard scientific method.
